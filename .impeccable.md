## Design Context

### Users
Researchers and developers who need to find historical AI events, papers, and models, understand their chronology, and follow source links. The creator confirmed this audience on 2026-10-05.

### Brand Personality
Restrained, precise, narrative. Build confidence through readable content and clear connections between events.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow VISUAL_GUIDE.md: Apple-inspired scrolling, strong information hierarchy, blue accents, spacious typography, and support for both light and dark themes. Preserve the existing visual language for incremental features.

### Design Principles
- Make dates, event categories, and source links easy to scan.
- Prioritize finding and comparing events; preserve filter state in shareable URLs.
- Offer a technology tree alongside chronology. Explain schools, methods, and architectures with cross-links where branches overlap, as requested by the creator on 2026-10-05.
- Use animation to support the narrative while keeping content visible and honoring reduced motion.
- Keep full functionality on mobile, with labeled controls and visible keyboard focus.
- Use consistent, complete Chinese and English interface copy.
